### P0.4 Self-monitoring раз в час (готовый скрипт 26 строк)

Критические метрики (только то, что ломает цепь):
- `systemctl is-active alf-gateway alf-filewatcher alf-healthcheck`
- `curl -sf :8452/health` (response time)
- `ls /root/.hermes/outbox/ | wc -l` (затор)
- `cat /root/.hermes/last_alf_response.txt` (последний ответ, не старше 3600s)
- `journalctl -u alf-gateway --since "1 hour ago" | grep -c ERROR`

```bash
#!/bin/bash
# /root/.hermes/scripts/selfmon.sh — cron: @hourly
LOG="/root/.hermes/logs/selfmon.log"
FAIL=0

echo "[$(date '+%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S')] selfmon start" >> "$LOG"

for srv in alf-gateway alf-filewatcher alf-healthcheck; do
  state=$(systemctl is-active "$srv" 2>/dev/null)
  echo "service $srv=$state" >> "$LOG"
  [ "$state" = "active" ] || FAIL=1
done

health=$(curl -sf -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}:%{time_total}s" :8452/health 2>/dev/null || echo "FAIL")
echo "health=$health" >> "$LOG"
[[ "$health" == 2* ]] || FAIL=1

outbox=$(ls -1 /root/.hermes/outbox/ 2>/dev/null | wc -l)
echo "outbox_count=$outbox" >> "$LOG"
[ "$outbox" -lt 50 ] || FAIL=1

last_response=$(cat /root/.hermes/last_alf_response.txt 2>/dev/null || echo "MISSING")
echo "last_alf_response=$last_response" >> "$LOG"
[ -n "$last_response" ] || FAIL=1

# telegram alert если FAIL
if [ "$FAIL" -eq 1 ]; then
  msg="ALF SELF-MONITOR FAIL $(date) — check $LOG"
  curl -s -X POST "https://api.telegram.org/bot$(grep TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN /root/.hermes/.env | cut -d= -f2)/sendMessage" \
    -d chat_id="$(grep ALERT_CHAT_ID /root/.hermes/.env | cut -d= -f2)" -d text="$msg"
fi
echo "selfmon exit=$FAIL" >> "$LOG"
```

Cron: `0 * * * * /root/.hermes/scripts/selfmon.sh`
